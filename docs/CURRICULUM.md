# Curriculum A1–C2

The course has 72 units: 12 per CEFR level. Standard German is the spine. Every unit also has a
**Schwiiz lane** (Swiss lane) that shows the same situation in dialect (Zurich German by default)
and teaches the Helvetisms of Swiss Standard German.

| Level | Hours (guide) | Swiss lane role |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| A1 | 80–120 | Listening and recognition only. Greetings, numbers, high-frequency verbs, Helvetisms. |
| A2 | 120–160 | Recognition. Introduces the missing Präteritum, *go* + infinitive, *-li* diminutives, dative possessives. |
| B1 | 160–200 | **Active dialect speaking begins** (small talk, phone calls, appointments). Bernese is offered as an alternative home dialect. |
| B2 | 180–220 | Fluent everyday dialect. Diglossia at work, media in dialect, first dialect songs. |
| C1 | 200–240 | Other dialects for listening: Basel, Lucerne, St. Gallen (and a Valais challenge). |
| C2 | open | Humour, idioms, wordplay, dialect song and literature, creative dialect writing. |

Key to each unit: **Goal** (communicative can-do), **Grammar**, **Vocabulary** (themes), and
**Swiss lane** (dialect and Helvetism focus). Grammar is cumulative: each unit revises the previous
unit's points in its exercises.

**Pronunciation** runs alongside the units as separate drills (`content/pronunciation/`), with at
least five `speak` items per unit from A1.7 on (read-aloud, shadowing of dialogue lines, intonation,
drill practice). The pronunciation syllabus per level is in [PRONUNCIATION.md](PRONUNCIATION.md).

## A1 – Ankommen (arriving)

Grammar spine: present tense, *sein/haben*, verb-second word order, gender and articles, accusative,
modal verbs, separable verbs, *nicht/kein*.

Bernese at A1: each unit has 2–4 **recognition** items (`variety: gsw-be`) and a few Bernese lines in the
Swiss-lane dialogue, usually Sandra (from Bern) talking to her family. Learners only listen and decode.

### A1.1 Grüezi und Hallo! – *Hello and hi!* ✅ written
- **Goal:** greet people, introduce yourself, say where you come from and live, and ask the same.
- **Grammar:** *sein* + personal pronouns; regular present tense (*wohnen, kommen, heißen*); *du* vs *Sie*; W-questions and verb second; yes/no questions with the verb first.
- **Vocabulary:** greetings and goodbyes, politeness, countries, cities, languages, question words.
- **Swiss lane:** Grüezi / Grüezi mitenand / Hoi / Sali; *ich bi, du bisch, er isch, mir sind*; *vo wo chunnsch?*; *z Züri*; articles before first names; *Duzis mache*; Merci.

### A1.2 Im Café – *At the café* ✅ written
- **Goal:** count to 100, understand prices in francs, order politely, and pay.
- **Grammar:** gender (*der/die/das*) and indefinite articles; accusative with *ein → einen*; *möchte(n)*, *Ich hätte gern*, *nehmen* (e → i); numbers 0–100.
- **Vocabulary:** café food and drink, money (Franken, Rappen), ordering and paying phrases.
- **Swiss lane:** *Was dörfs sii?*, *Ich hett gärn ...*; Kafi, Schale, Gipfeli, Weggli, Mineral, Stange; Zurich numbers (drü, föif, sächs, nüün, zää, zwänzg, füfzg); Swiss German has no separate accusative article (*en Kafi*); *zäme*, *Isch guet so*. Helvetisms: Gipfeli, Glace, Morgenessen, Poulet.

### A1.3 Meine Familie – *My family* ✅ written
- **Goal:** talk about your family, say names and ages, and ask about other people's families.
- **Grammar:** *haben*; possessive articles (*mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer, Ihr*) in the nominative and the masculine accusative; plural nouns; age with *sein*.
- **Vocabulary:** family members, marital status, pets, adjectives *alt, klein, groß, süß*.
- **Swiss lane:** *ich ha, du häsch, er hät, mir händ*; *min/mini/mis*; Muetter, Brueder, Schwöschter, Gschwüschterti, Chind; Götti / Gotte; *herzig*; plural verbs in *-ed*.

### A1.4 Mein Tag – *My day* ✅ written
- **Goal:** describe your daily routine, tell the time, and say when you do things.
- **Grammar:** stem-changing verbs (*fahren, schlafen, essen, lesen, sehen*); separable verbs I (*aufstehen, anfangen, einkaufen*); time prepositions *um, am, von ... bis*; inversion after time expressions (*Um sieben stehe ich auf*).
- **Vocabulary:** daily routine, clock times, days of the week, parts of the day, meals.
- **Swiss lane:** clock times (*halbi drü* = halb drei, *Viertel ab / Viertel vor*); Zmorge, Zmittag, Znacht, Znüni, Zvieri; *ich stah uf*; *am Morge, am Abig*.

### A1.5 Einkaufen – *Grocery shopping* ✅ written
- **Goal:** buy food, ask for quantities and prices, and say what you don't want.
- **Grammar:** negation with *nicht* vs *kein*; definite accusative (*den*); *ja / nein / doch*; quantities (*ein Kilo, eine Flasche, 200 Gramm*).
- **Vocabulary:** groceries, fruit and vegetables, packaging, weights, the supermarket.
- **Swiss lane:** *poschte*, Migros/Coop culture, Härdöpfel, Rüebli, Peperoni, Rahm; *nöd* and *kei*; *moll* (= doch); *Säckli*; paying with Twint.

### A1.6 Wohnen in der Schweiz – *Living in Switzerland* ✅ written
- **Goal:** describe your flat and your neighbourhood, and understand a simple flat ad.
- **Grammar:** *es gibt* + accusative; predicative adjectives; definite articles in the accusative; location as fixed chunks (*in der Küche, im Wohnzimmer*).
- **Vocabulary:** rooms, furniture, household appliances, the building, rent (Miete, Nebenkosten).
- **Swiss lane:** Wohnig, Chuchi, Stube, Stäge; the shared *Waschküche* and its rules; Güsel / Güselsack; the Swiss "3½-Zimmer-Wohnung" (ch-std room counting).

### A1.7 Freizeit und Hobbys – *Free time and hobbies* ✅ written
- **Goal:** talk about hobbies and preferences, say what you can and want to do, and talk about the seasons and the weather.
- **Grammar:** *gern / lieber / am liebsten* (verb + *gern*; *Ich mag* + noun as a chunk); modal verbs *können, wollen* and the sentence bracket (*Ich kann nicht Ski fahren*); *ich will* ≠ *I will*; weather with *es* (*es regnet, es ist kalt*) and *im Sommer*.
- **Vocabulary:** hobbies, sports, music, the cinema, seasons, basic weather, frequency (*oft, manchmal, nie*).
- **Swiss lane:** *chöne* (*ich chan, du chasch, mir chönd*), *wele* (*ich wott, du wottsch, mir wänd*); *mega*, *gärn*, *gar nöd*; schwümme, wandere, Ski fahre, jasse (the Swiss card game Jass), *is Kino*, *gömmer*; the Badi; *Summer, Sunne, Wätter*. Bernese: *mir wei, i cha, mir chöi*, swimming in the Aare. Helvetisms: Velo fahren, Badi, grillieren, der Match.
- **Pronunciation:** ü (*Frühling*), ich-/ach-Laut, w/v; yes/no question intonation; shadowing of the intro dialogue.

### A1.8 Unterwegs in der Stadt – *Getting around town* ✅ written
- **Goal:** ask for and understand directions, use public transport, and buy a ticket.
- **Grammar:** imperative (*Sie, du, ihr*; *Nimm!*, *Fahr!*, separable *Steig aus!*); modal verbs *müssen, dürfen* (*nicht müssen* = don't have to vs *nicht dürfen* = mustn't; *man darf*); *mit dem Bus / mit dem Tram*, *zum / zur*, *zu Fuß* as chunks; ordinal numbers for directions (*die zweite Straße, an der dritten Haltestelle*).
- **Vocabulary:** places in town, transport, directions, tickets and timetables, *einsteigen / aussteigen / umsteigen*.
- **Swiss lane:** es Tram, Velo, Billett, Trottoir, Perron (ch-std for Bahnsteig), Halbtax, GA; *Lueg emal!*, *Nimm s Tram!*, *grad us* (straight on), *links / rächts*; *du muesch nöd* vs *du dörfsch nöd*. The intro dialogue's passer-by speaks Swiss Standard German (ch-std lines). Bernese: *Pass uf, gäu!*, *linggs*, *ds Tram*.
- **Pronunciation:** z = ts, st/sp = scht/schp, compound stress (*BAHN-hof*); shadowing a Swiss Standard speaker.

### A1.9 Termine und Verabredungen – *Appointments and plans* ✅ written
- **Goal:** make, change and cancel an appointment, also on the phone, and give the date.
- **Grammar:** separable verbs II (*anrufen, vorbeikommen, absagen, mitbringen*), also with modal verbs (*Ich muss den Termin absagen*); inseparable *verschieben*; *sollen* (instructions from others, *Soll ich ...?* offers) vs *möchte*; dates with ordinal numbers (*der dritte Mai, am dritten Mai*); time adverbs (*morgen, übermorgen, nächste Woche, morgen früh*).
- **Vocabulary:** months, dates, the phone (*Auf Wiederhören*), the doctor's and hairdresser's appointment, invitations.
- **Swiss lane:** *aalüüte* (*Ich lüüt dir aa* – dative); *Häsch morn Ziit?*; *morn, übermorn*; *verbii cho*, *Söll ich öppis mitbringe?*, *abgmacht*; the phone greeting with your surname; Natel/Handy, Coiffeur, Agenda; months (*Auguscht, Septämber*), 1 August. Bernese: *het, dr Ätti, Lüt mer aa!*
- **Pronunciation:** stress of separable vs inseparable prefixes (*AN-rufen* vs *ver-SCHIE-ben*), dates aloud; shadowing a Swiss Standard receptionist.

### A1.10 Kleidung und Wetter – *Clothes and weather*
- **Goal:** buy clothes, talk about size, colour and price, and describe the weather.
- **Grammar:** personal pronouns in the accusative (*mich, dich, ihn, sie, es, uns, euch*); *welch- / dies-*; colours; adjectives after *sein*; *zu* + adjective (*zu groß*).
- **Vocabulary:** clothes, colours, sizes, weather, seasons.
- **Swiss lane:** *chalt / heiss*; *Es schiffet* (it's pouring); Jäggli, Finke (slippers), Pulli; *Das staht dir guet*; *tüür*.

### A1.11 Gesundheit – *Health*
- **Goal:** say what hurts, make a doctor's appointment, and understand simple instructions at the pharmacy.
- **Grammar:** modal verbs review (*müssen, dürfen, sollen*); imperative in instructions; *weh tun* and *Ich habe ...schmerzen* as chunks.
- **Vocabulary:** body parts, illnesses, the pharmacy, the doctor's practice.
- **Swiss lane:** *Ich ha Chopfweh / Buuchweh*; Spital (ch-std), Apotheke, *Gsundheit!*; Krankenkasse as a word to recognise; *müed, chrank*.

### A1.12 Feste und Feiern – *Celebrations* (A1 review)
- **Goal:** invite, accept or decline, and congratulate people; review of A1.
- **Grammar:** review of verb second, the sentence bracket (modal and separable verbs), *nicht/kein*, accusative and questions.
- **Vocabulary:** birthdays, holidays, invitations, presents, wishes.
- **Swiss lane:** Geburi, Fäscht, *Vill Glück zum Geburtstag!*; 1. August (national day), Samichlaus (6 December), Sechseläuten (Zurich); *En Guete!*, *Proscht!*

## A2 – Sich zurechtfinden (finding your way)

Grammar spine: Perfekt, dative, two-way prepositions, comparatives, *weil/dass/wenn*, reflexive verbs.
Swiss lane: introduces the absence of the Präteritum, *go* + infinitive, *-li* diminutives.

### A2.1 Wie war das Wochenende? – *How was the weekend?*
- **Goal:** tell what you did at the weekend.
- **Grammar:** Perfekt with *haben*; regular participles (*ge-...-t*); participles of separable verbs (*eingekauft*); time markers (*gestern, letztes Wochenende*).
- **Vocabulary:** weekend activities, household chores, time expressions for the past.
- **Swiss lane:** **Swiss German has no Präteritum.** Every past is Perfekt: *ich ha gmacht, ich bi gsii, ich ha gha*. Participles without *e*: *gmacht, gchauft, gseit*.

### A2.2 Reisen und Ferien – *Travel and holidays*
- **Goal:** talk about past trips, and book a room or a ticket.
- **Grammar:** Perfekt with *sein* (motion and change); irregular participles; Präteritum of *sein* and *haben* (*war, hatte*).
- **Vocabulary:** travel, accommodation, holidays, landscapes.
- **Swiss lane:** Ferien (ch-std for Urlaub); *Mir sind is Tessin gfahre*; *gsii* instead of *war*; the Swiss rail network as a topic.

### A2.3 Arbeit und Beruf – *Work and jobs*
- **Goal:** describe your job and workplace, and understand a job ad.
- **Grammar:** the dative: articles and endings; prepositions with the dative (*mit, bei, von, zu, nach, aus, seit*); dative pronouns.
- **Vocabulary:** jobs, the workplace, working hours, colleagues.
- **Swiss lane:** *schaffe*, Büez, Lehre / Lehrling (apprenticeship), Chef, Kolleg; *bim* (beim), *am* / *im* contractions; Znüni-Pause culture.

### A2.4 Geschenke und Einladungen – *Presents and invitations*
- **Goal:** give and receive presents, and say what you like and what suits someone.
- **Grammar:** verbs with dative (*helfen, gefallen, gehören, schmecken*); verbs with dative + accusative (*geben, schenken*); order of the objects.
- **Vocabulary:** presents, parties, food at parties, compliments.
- **Swiss lane:** *Das gfallt mer*; dative pronouns *mir / dir / em / ere*; *Das isch für dich*; *Merci, das wär doch nöd nötig gsii!*

### A2.5 Umziehen und Einrichten – *Moving and furnishing*
- **Goal:** say where things are and where they go, and plan a move.
- **Grammar:** two-way prepositions (*in, an, auf, über, unter, vor, hinter, neben, zwischen*): *Wo?* + dative, *Wohin?* + accusative; *stellen/stehen, legen/liegen, hängen*.
- **Vocabulary:** furniture, the move, household objects.
- **Swiss lane:** **-li diminutives**: Tischli, Hüsli, Chätzli, Bitzeli, Müsli. They are neuter and very frequent, and not always "small". Also *ufe / abe / ine / use* (direction).

### A2.6 Vergleichen und Kaufen – *Comparing and buying*
- **Goal:** compare products and prices, and make a choice.
- **Grammar:** comparative and superlative (*größer, am größten*; irregular *gut – besser – am besten*); *als* vs *wie*.
- **Vocabulary:** shopping, electronics, quality, price, sales.
- **Swiss lane:** *gröösser, am billigschte*; Aktion, Ausverkauf; *tüür / günschtig*; *das isch gäbig*.

### A2.7 Gesund leben – *Living healthily*
- **Goal:** talk about routines, fitness and well-being, and give simple advice.
- **Grammar:** reflexive verbs (*sich waschen, sich fühlen, sich freuen*); reflexive pronouns in the accusative; *sich* + dative with body parts (preview).
- **Vocabulary:** sport, food, sleep, stress, the body.
- **Swiss lane:** **go + infinitive**: *Ich gang go jogge*, *Mir gönd go poschte*; *sich fröie*; *müed, gstresst*.

### A2.8 Schule und Lernen – *School and learning*
- **Goal:** talk about your education and give reasons.
- **Grammar:** subordinate clauses with *weil, dass*; verb-final word order; *denn* vs *weil*.
- **Vocabulary:** school, subjects, exams, languages, learning strategies.
- **Swiss lane:** the Swiss school system (Kindergarte, Primar, Sek, Gymi, Lehre); *wil* (weil), *dass*; Matur (ch-std for Abitur).

### A2.9 Feste und Bräuche – *Customs and traditions*
- **Goal:** describe a festival or tradition from your country and from Switzerland.
- **Grammar:** *wenn* (repeated/present) vs *als* (single past event); *wenn* for conditions; ordinal numbers and dates (review).
- **Vocabulary:** festivals, seasons, customs, food at festivals.
- **Swiss lane:** Fasnacht, Räbeliechtli, Zibelemärit (Bern), Chlausjage; *wänn* in dialect is used for both *wenn* and *als*.

### A2.10 Medien und Kommunikation – *Media and messaging*
- **Goal:** talk about how you use media, write messages and short emails.
- **Grammar:** indirect questions (*ob*, W-word + verb-final); connectors *deshalb, trotzdem, dann*; *zu* + infinitive (basic).
- **Vocabulary:** phones, apps, the internet, emails, the news.
- **Swiss lane:** how Swiss people **text** in dialect (WhatsApp spelling, abbreviations such as *hdl*, *ok, bis morn*); *Händ en schöne Abig*; recognising written dialect.

### A2.11 Natur und Umwelt – *Nature and the environment*
- **Goal:** talk about nature, weekends outdoors and recycling.
- **Grammar:** polite requests with *würde* and *könnte* (as chunks); indefinite pronouns (*man, jemand, niemand*); review of the dative.
- **Vocabulary:** landscape, animals, weather, recycling, rubbish.
- **Swiss lane:** recycling rules (PET, Altglas, Güselsack, Grüngut); Bärg, See, wandere; *öpper, niemert, öppis, nüt*.

### A2.12 Unterwegs in der Schweiz – *Around Switzerland* (A2 review)
- **Goal:** plan and describe a trip in Switzerland; review of A2.
- **Grammar:** review of Perfekt, dative, two-way prepositions and subordinate clauses.
- **Vocabulary:** cantons, cities, the train, sights, regional food.
- **Swiss lane:** first comparison of regional greetings and words (Zurich *Grüezi*, Bern *Grüessech*, Basel *Grüezi / Hoi*); listening to a short Bernese sample as a preview.

## B1 – Mitreden (joining in)

Grammar spine: Präteritum, genitive, relative clauses, present passive, Konjunktiv II, adjective endings.
Swiss lane: **active dialect speaking starts**. Every unit has dialect *speak* items and a Reto
roleplay answered in dialect. **Bernese** (gsw-be) is available as the home dialect from B1.

### B1.1 Lebensgeschichten – *Life stories*
- **Goal:** tell your life story and understand short biographies.
- **Grammar:** Präteritum of regular and irregular verbs; Präteritum (written) vs Perfekt (spoken); *seit / vor / nach*.
- **Vocabulary:** biography, life events, school and career milestones.
- **Swiss lane:** speak about your life in dialect using Perfekt only; introductions and small talk in Zurich German (first active speaking); Bernese option: *i bi gsi*.

### B1.2 Menschen beschreiben – *Describing people*
- **Goal:** describe the appearance and character of people.
- **Grammar:** adjective endings after *der/ein*/no article (nominative and accusative).
- **Vocabulary:** appearance, character, feelings, relationships.
- **Swiss lane:** adjective endings in dialect (simpler: *en nette Maa, e netti Frau, es nätts Chind*); *gmüetlich, lieb, herzig, wüescht*; describe a friend in dialect.

### B1.3 Wohnungssuche – *Looking for a flat*
- **Goal:** understand flat ads, phone a landlord, and ask for a viewing.
- **Grammar:** relative clauses (relative pronouns in the nominative, accusative and dative); prepositions + relative pronoun.
- **Vocabulary:** flat ads, contracts, rent, neighbours, the viewing.
- **Swiss lane:** phone call to a Verwaltung in dialect; *wo* as the universal dialect relative pronoun (*de Maa, wo da wohnt*); Bernese option: *das Hüsli, wo mir wohne*.

### B1.4 Im Büro – *At the office*
- **Goal:** small talk at work, organising tasks, and writing a short work email.
- **Grammar:** the genitive (articles, *-s/-es*); *wegen, trotz, während, statt*; adjective endings in the dative and genitive.
- **Vocabulary:** office, tasks, meetings, the team, emails.
- **Swiss lane:** the genitive does not exist in dialect: *em Reto sis Auto*, *s Auto vom Reto*; coffee-break small talk; when colleagues switch to Hochdeutsch.

### B1.5 Wünsche und Träume – *Wishes and dreams*
- **Goal:** talk about wishes, dreams and unreal situations.
- **Grammar:** Konjunktiv II (*würde, wäre, hätte, könnte, müsste*); unreal wishes and conditions (present).
- **Vocabulary:** dreams, plans, lifestyle, lottery, travel.
- **Swiss lane:** dialect Konjunktiv: *ich wär, ich hett, ich chönnt, ich wür, ich wett* (I would like); speak about your dream life in dialect.

### B1.6 Rat und Hilfe – *Advice and help*
- **Goal:** describe a problem, ask for advice, and give advice politely.
- **Grammar:** Konjunktiv II for advice and polite requests (*Du solltest ..., Könnten Sie ...?*); *an deiner Stelle*.
- **Vocabulary:** everyday problems, services, repairs, complaints.
- **Swiss lane:** polite requests in dialect (*Chönnted Sie mer hälfe?*); Bernese polite form (*Chöit Dir mer hälfe?*); calling a repair service.

### B1.7 Technik im Alltag – *Technology in everyday life*
- **Goal:** explain how something works or is made, and describe processes.
- **Grammar:** present passive (*werden* + participle); passive with modal verbs; *von* + agent.
- **Vocabulary:** devices, apps, instructions, production.
- **Swiss lane:** passive in dialect (*das wird gmacht*, *das mues repariert wärde*); explain an app to Reto in dialect.

### B1.8 Feste und Kultur – *Festivals and culture*
- **Goal:** describe and compare cultural events in detail.
- **Grammar:** temporal clauses (*als, wenn, während, bevor, nachdem*), with Plusquamperfekt as a preview with *nachdem*.
- **Vocabulary:** culture, concerts, museums, festivals, traditions.
- **Swiss lane:** describe a Swiss festival in dialect; Bernese lane: Zibelemärit, Bärner Fasnacht; *wo* = *als* in dialect narration.

### B1.9 Mobilität und Umwelt – *Mobility and the environment*
- **Goal:** discuss transport choices and environmental habits.
- **Grammar:** infinitive clauses (*zu*, *um ... zu*, *ohne ... zu*); *damit*.
- **Vocabulary:** public transport, cycling, energy, climate, everyday ecology.
- **Swiss lane:** *zum ... (go)* in dialect for purpose (*Ich gang uf Bern go schaffe*); talk about Velo vs ÖV in dialect.

### B1.10 Zusammenleben – *Living together*
- **Goal:** discuss rules in the building and in society, and solve a conflict with a neighbour.
- **Grammar:** *obwohl / trotzdem*, *je ... desto*; n-declension (*den Nachbarn, dem Kollegen*).
- **Vocabulary:** house rules, neighbours, noise, community, voting basics.
- **Swiss lane:** the Hausordnung conversation in dialect; Swiss *Ruhezeit*; Abstimmung, Gemeinde (ch-std political words).

### B1.11 Meinungen – *Opinions*
- **Goal:** give and justify opinions, agree and disagree.
- **Grammar:** verbs with prepositions (*sich interessieren für, denken an*); *da-* and *wo-* compounds.
- **Vocabulary:** media, debates, social topics, opinion phrases.
- **Swiss lane:** giving opinions in dialect (*Ich find ..., Ich bi nöd eiverstande*); the tag *oder?*; a phone discussion with a friend.

### B1.12 Mein Leben in der Schweiz – *My life in Switzerland* (B1 review)
- **Goal:** manage official and everyday tasks independently; review of B1 and exam strategies.
- **Grammar:** review of Präteritum, relative clauses, passive, Konjunktiv II and adjective endings.
- **Vocabulary:** the Gemeinde, permits, insurance, banks, the post office.
- **Swiss lane:** appointments and phone calls with offices in dialect; Zurich vs Bernese comparison (*nöd / nid*, *mir händ / mir hei*).

## B2 – Überzeugen (making your case)

Grammar spine: passive in all tenses, Konjunktiv I, participial clauses, nominalisation, connectors.
Swiss lane: fluent everyday dialect, diglossia at work, dialect in the media, first songs.

### B2.1 Arbeit im Wandel – *The changing world of work*
- **Goal:** discuss trends in work and describe changes.
- **Grammar:** passive in Präteritum, Perfekt and Plusquamperfekt; Zustandspassiv (*ist geöffnet*).
- **Vocabulary:** remote work, automation, careers, the labour market.
- **Swiss lane:** diglossia at work: when Swiss colleagues switch to Hochdeutsch (meetings, emails, foreigners present); practise switching yourself.

### B2.2 Bildung und Karriere – *Education and career*
- **Goal:** compare education systems and plan a career step.
- **Grammar:** alternatives to the passive (*man*, *sich lassen*, *-bar*, *sein + zu*); passive with modal verbs in all tenses.
- **Vocabulary:** degrees, apprenticeships, further training, applications.
- **Swiss lane:** the dual system (Lehre, Berufsmatur, Fachhochschule); a job interview small talk in dialect; ch-std *Matur, Diplom*.

### B2.3 Wissenschaft – *Science and research*
- **Goal:** understand and summarise popular-science texts.
- **Grammar:** nominalisation of verbs and adjectives (*die Entwicklung, das Wachstum*); introduction to nominal style.
- **Vocabulary:** research, experiments, data, discoveries.
- **Swiss lane:** dialect avoids nominal style, so you rephrase verbally; ETH and EPFL; explain a study to Reto in dialect.

### B2.4 Nachrichten – *The news*
- **Goal:** report what others said and understand news reports.
- **Grammar:** Konjunktiv I for indirect speech; Konjunktiv II as a substitute.
- **Vocabulary:** news, politics, reports, quotes.
- **Swiss lane:** SRF news in Standard vs talk and call-in shows in dialect; dialect reported speech (*Er hät gseit, er heig kei Ziit*; Zurich Konjunktiv I *heig*, also heard as *heg*).

### B2.5 Wirtschaft und Konsum – *Economy and consumption*
- **Goal:** discuss consumer behaviour, money and advertising.
- **Grammar:** two-part connectors (*sowohl ... als auch, weder ... noch, entweder ... oder, zwar ... aber, nicht nur ... sondern auch*).
- **Vocabulary:** money, banking, advertising, consumer rights.
- **Swiss lane:** money talk in dialect (*Stutz, Chlütter*); Zurich vs Bernese listening at the market.

### B2.6 Politik und direkte Demokratie – *Politics and direct democracy*
- **Goal:** explain a political system and argue for or against a proposal.
- **Grammar:** Partizip I and II as adjectives; participial clauses.
- **Vocabulary:** voting, initiatives, referendums, parties, the federal system.
- **Swiss lane:** Abstimmung, Initiative, Referendum, Gemeindeversammlung, Vernehmlassung (ch-std); a political chat at the Stammtisch in dialect.

### B2.7 Gesundheitssystem – *Healthcare*
- **Goal:** discuss healthcare and insurance, and deal with a medical appointment.
- **Grammar:** subjective use of modal verbs (*soll, muss, kann, dürfte*); Futur I and II for assumptions.
- **Vocabulary:** insurance, treatment, prevention, health policy.
- **Swiss lane:** Krankenkasse, Franchise, Hausarzt; a doctor's appointment entirely in dialect.

### B2.8 Kunst und Kultur – *Art and culture*
- **Goal:** describe and review a book, film or exhibition.
- **Grammar:** extended relative clauses (*wer, was, wo*; *dessen / deren*); relative clauses with prepositions.
- **Vocabulary:** literature, film, art, criticism.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss literature in Standard (Dürrenmatt, Frisch); first dialect song for listening: Mani Matter (Bernese chansons).

### B2.9 Klima und Umwelt – *Climate and the environment*
- **Goal:** argue in writing and in a discussion on environmental issues.
- **Grammar:** causal, concessive and consecutive connectors (*infolgedessen, dennoch, sodass, folglich*); argumentation structure.
- **Vocabulary:** climate, energy, policy, glaciers, sustainability.
- **Swiss lane:** debating in dialect (*Ich gseh das andersch*); Swiss topics such as glaciers and energy.

### B2.10 Kommunikation – *Relationships and communication*
- **Goal:** handle conflicts and give feedback diplomatically.
- **Grammar:** verbs, adjectives and nouns with prepositions; introduction to *Funktionsverbgefüge* (*zur Verfügung stellen*).
- **Vocabulary:** feelings, conflict, feedback, relationships.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss indirectness and politeness; Du/Sie norms; Bernese *Ihrzen* (*Dir*).

### B2.11 Migration und Identität – *Migration and identity*
- **Goal:** talk about identity, belonging and multilingualism.
- **Grammar:** Konjunktiv II in the past (*hätte gemacht, wäre gegangen*); unreal conditions in the past.
- **Vocabulary:** migration, integration, languages, identity.
- **Swiss lane:** the four national languages, the Röstigraben, French and Italian loanwords in dialect (*Merci, Exgüsi, Glace, Coiffeur*).

### B2.12 B2-Abschluss – *B2 review*
- **Goal:** write a formal letter and an argumentative essay, and give a presentation; review of B2.
- **Grammar:** review of passive, Konjunktiv I and II, connectors and nominalisation.
- **Vocabulary:** text types and presentation phrases.
- **Swiss lane:** an extended speaking task in dialect (a 3-minute talk about your life in Switzerland).

## C1 – Feinheiten (nuance)

Grammar spine: extended attributes, nominal style, modal particles, register.
Swiss lane: listening to other dialects (Basel, Lucerne, St. Gallen), Swiss officialese, Swiss particles.

### C1.1 Sprache und Register – *Language and register*
- **Goal:** recognise and use different registers.
- **Grammar:** register markers; modal particles I (*doch, ja, mal, eben, halt*).
- **Vocabulary:** language varieties, slang, formal speech.
- **Swiss lane:** **Basel German** listening (*Baseldytsch*: initial *k* instead of *ch* (*Kind, Kuchi*), *mir sinn*); a comparison with Zurich.

### C1.2 Akademisches Schreiben – *Academic writing*
- **Goal:** write and understand academic texts.
- **Grammar:** extended participial attributes (*die seit Jahren steigenden Preise*).
- **Vocabulary:** academic phrases, citation, argument structure.
- **Swiss lane:** academic Swiss Standard German vs German usage; ch-std conventions (*innert, allfällig, vorgängig*).

### C1.3 Globale Wirtschaft – *The global economy*
- **Goal:** analyse economic texts and present data.
- **Grammar:** turning nominal style into verbal style and back.
- **Vocabulary:** markets, trade, finance, statistics.
- **Swiss lane:** **Lucerne German** listening (business conversation); Swiss economic terms.

### C1.4 Recht und Verwaltung – *Law and administration*
- **Goal:** understand official letters and legal texts, and write formal objections.
- **Grammar:** *Funktionsverbgefüge*; passive and *sein + zu* in legal texts; officialese.
- **Vocabulary:** contracts, authorities, rights, procedures.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss officialese (ch-std): Kanton, Gemeinde, Bewilligung, Einsprache, Busse; reading a Swiss official letter.

### C1.5 Medien und Manipulation – *Media and manipulation*
- **Goal:** analyse media bias and reported claims.
- **Grammar:** Konjunktiv I and II for distance; *sollen / wollen* for claims (*Er will es gesehen haben*).
- **Vocabulary:** media, propaganda, fact-checking, rhetoric.
- **Swiss lane:** **St. Gallen German** listening (radio interview); its fast speech and characteristic vowels.

### C1.6 Psychologie – *Psychology and behaviour*
- **Goal:** discuss behaviour and emotions with nuance.
- **Grammar:** modal particles II (*wohl, schon, eigentlich, denn, ruhig, bloß*).
- **Vocabulary:** psychology, emotions, personality, habits.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss particles and fillers (*äbe, halt, gäll, oder, scho*), and their pragmatic effect.

### C1.7 Technik und Ethik – *Technology and ethics*
- **Goal:** take part in a complex debate.
- **Grammar:** adversative and concessive clauses (*wohingegen, während, wenngleich, zumal*); complex sentence structures.
- **Vocabulary:** AI, data protection, bioethics, responsibility.
- **Swiss lane:** a debate recording mixing Basel and Zurich speakers; follow the argument across dialects.

### C1.8 Geschichte – *History*
- **Goal:** narrate and analyse historical events.
- **Grammar:** Präteritum narration, Plusquamperfekt, narrative connectors.
- **Vocabulary:** history, politics, wars and treaties, memory.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss history (1291, 1848, neutrality); a Lucerne storyteller in dialect.

### C1.9 Film und Literatur – *Film and literature*
- **Goal:** interpret literary texts and films.
- **Grammar:** word order for emphasis (Vorfeld, Nachfeld, Ausklammerung).
- **Vocabulary:** literary analysis, narrative techniques, film criticism.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss films in dialect; a **Valais German** listening challenge (Walliserdütsch).

### C1.10 Professionell kommunizieren – *Professional communication*
- **Goal:** negotiate, moderate meetings and write professional emails.
- **Grammar:** politeness strategies with Konjunktiv II, hedging, indirect requests.
- **Vocabulary:** negotiation, meetings, project management.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss business etiquette; switching between dialect and Standard in the same meeting.

### C1.11 Wissen vermitteln – *Explaining knowledge*
- **Goal:** give a clear talk on a complex topic for non-experts.
- **Grammar:** switching between nominal and verbal style for different audiences; presenting data.
- **Vocabulary:** presentation, visualisation, popularisation.
- **Swiss lane:** eastern Swiss accents (St. Gallen, Graubünden) and Romansh loanwords.

### C1.12 C1-Abschluss – *C1 review*
- **Goal:** review of C1 and exam strategies.
- **Grammar:** review of extended attributes, nominal style, particles and register.
- **Vocabulary:** review.
- **Swiss lane:** mixed-dialect listening test (Basel, Lucerne, St. Gallen, Bern, Zurich).

## C2 – Meisterschaft (mastery)

Grammar spine: stylistics, idioms, wordplay. Swiss lane: humour, idioms, dialect song and literature.

### C2.1 Redewendungen – *Idioms*
- **Goal:** understand and use idioms appropriately.
- **Grammar:** idiomatic and figurative language, fixed expressions and their variation.
- **Vocabulary:** idioms by theme (body, animals, food).
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss idioms (*Das isch nöd mis Bier*, *de Foifer und s Weggli wele*, *Es isch mer gliich*).

### C2.2 Stil und Stilmittel – *Style and rhetorical devices*
- **Goal:** analyse and use stylistic devices.
- **Grammar:** metaphor, irony, understatement, rhythm in prose.
- **Vocabulary:** stylistics.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss cabaret and comedy for listening (Emil Steinberger, Swiss satire shows); understatement as Swiss humour.

### C2.3 Wortspiele und Humor – *Wordplay and humour*
- **Goal:** understand and create puns and jokes.
- **Grammar:** ambiguity, homophones, word formation for effect.
- **Vocabulary:** humour.
- **Swiss lane:** dialect puns; Mani Matter's wordplay (Bernese).

### C2.4 Literatur – *Literature*
- **Goal:** read and discuss literary texts in depth.
- **Grammar:** literary and archaic forms; Konjunktiv in literature.
- **Vocabulary:** literary criticism.
- **Swiss lane:** Mundartliteratur: Kurt Marti, Pedro Lenz (*Der Goalie bin ig*, Bernese), the spoken-word scene.

### C2.5 Essay und Feuilleton – *Essays and features*
- **Goal:** write a polished essay or feature article.
- **Grammar:** essayistic style, register mixing for effect.
- **Vocabulary:** culture journalism.
- **Swiss lane:** NZZ / Swiss feuilleton style (ch-std); Swiss vs German journalistic conventions.

### C2.6 Sprachgeschichte und Varietäten – *Language history and varieties*
- **Goal:** discuss the history and variation of German.
- **Grammar:** historical forms, sound shifts, standardisation.
- **Vocabulary:** linguistics.
- **Swiss lane:** the Alemannic dialects, the dialect continuum, Dieth spelling, why Swiss German has no Präteritum.

### C2.7 Rhetorik und Debatte – *Rhetoric and debate*
- **Goal:** give a persuasive speech and win a debate.
- **Grammar:** rhetorical structure, register shifts, emphasis.
- **Vocabulary:** rhetoric, politics.
- **Swiss lane:** speeches in the Swiss parliament; dialect in political campaigning.

### C2.8 Abstraktes Denken – *Law, philosophy, abstraction*
- **Goal:** handle highly abstract texts precisely.
- **Grammar:** abstract nominal style, precision and hedging.
- **Vocabulary:** philosophy, law.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss legal language (ZGB, Bundesverfassung), its clarity tradition (ch-std).

### C2.9 Musik und Lyrik – *Music and poetry*
- **Goal:** interpret poems and song lyrics.
- **Grammar:** poetic licence, rhythm, sound symbolism.
- **Vocabulary:** music and poetry.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss dialect pop and rap for listening (Bernese and Zurich artists); licensed excerpts only.

### C2.10 Interkulturelle Kompetenz – *Intercultural competence*
- **Goal:** mediate between cultures and handle subtle pragmatics.
- **Grammar:** pragmatics, politeness theory, implicature.
- **Vocabulary:** intercultural communication.
- **Swiss lane:** Swiss German identity, the Röstigraben, when foreigners should and shouldn't speak dialect.

### C2.11 Kreatives Schreiben – *Creative writing*
- **Goal:** write creative texts, parody and pastiche.
- **Grammar:** style imitation, narrative voice.
- **Vocabulary:** creative writing.
- **Swiss lane:** write a short dialect text in Dieth spelling (a story or a column).

### C2.12 Meisterstück – *Masterpiece* (C2 review)
- **Goal:** a final project and review of C2.
- **Grammar:** review.
- **Vocabulary:** review.
