## 1. What Doppelpunkt is

Doppelpunkt is a course for Standard German and Swiss German (levels A1 to C2) that runs in your
browser and works offline. Level A1 is free. Levels A2 and above can be unlocked one by one, all at
once with a yearly pass, or with an access code printed in a Doppelpunkt book. Unit 1 of every
level can be tried for free.

## 2. No account; your device

You don't need an account. Your progress and your unlocks are stored on your device. If you delete
the app's data, reset the app or lose the device, your progress is gone unless you exported it. You
can restore purchases (section 5).

## 5. Restoring purchases

Each purchase has a purchase id (on your Stripe receipt). With it and the email address you used,
you can restore the unlock on another device under **Unlock → Restore a purchase**. You can also
copy the unlock token from Settings and paste it on another device. Unlocks are for your personal
use; don't share tokens or codes publicly. We may block tokens or codes that are shared publicly.
