# Native-speaker review queue

Content in `content/` was drafted without a native review. Before v1.0 every unit needs sign-off
from a German native teacher and, for every Swiss lane, a Zurich German native (Bernese forms from
a Bernese speaker). Sign-off is recorded in each unit's `review` field (`de`, `ch`, `date`); the open
questions for the reviewer are in `review.notes`. No unit has a human sign-off yet.

**Nothing on this page is a native review.** The AI passes catch clear errors and record the reasoning
and the sources, so the human reviewer can work faster. Every line still needs a native tick.

Legend: ✅ confirmed, no change · ✏️ corrected in content · ❓ no source found, still open

## Open format questions
- A1 units 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6 use `transform` from `de` to `de` (du → Sie, word order, negation). The UI
  labels it "Rewrite in Hochdeutsch".
- Listening MCQs refer to the dialogues until studio audio exists (v0.2).
- Bernese items use the infix `-ch-be-` in their ids (`a1-u04-ch-be-match-days`). They are recognition
  items at A1, ahead of the B1 Bernese option in the curriculum, and are kept to 3 per unit.
- Units 8 and 9 tag Swiss speakers who speak Standard German (passer-by, receptionist) as `ch-std`
  dialogue lines inside the `de` intro dialogue, with Swiss spelling (*zu Fuss, Strasse*).
- Swiss pronunciation drills (`gsw-ch-gg-kch`, `gsw-vowels`) are A1 listening-and-imitation drills,
  ahead of active dialect speaking at B1. Their pairs contrast dialect words only; the Standard German
  word is given in the gloss.
